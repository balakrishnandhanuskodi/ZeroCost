Project Name

FinPilot AI – Smart Personal Finance & Loan Planner

Objective

Design a modern, premium, intuitive Personal Finance Management application that helps individuals manage their complete financial life—not just expenses.

The application should assist users in:

Tracking income and expenses
Planning monthly budgets
Managing multiple loans and EMIs
Understanding principal vs. interest paid
Forecasting cash flow
Improving savings
Becoming debt-free faster
Receiving AI-powered financial insights

The UI should feel like a combination of Notion + Apple Wallet + Revolut + CRED + Google Material Design, with a premium fintech aesthetic.

Final Design Goal

Create a world-class personal finance platform that feels as polished as CRED, Revolut, Apple Wallet, and Notion, but is specifically designed to help users understand their finances, eliminate debt, grow savings, and make smarter financial decisions. The interface should be visually rich, highly interactive, and optimized for both web and mobile, making complex financial information easy to understand and act upon.
